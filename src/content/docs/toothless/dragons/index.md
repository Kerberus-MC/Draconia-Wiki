## Variant Stat Bonuses

Alpha and Titanwing variants (including the Mountain King Goregutter) get the following stat increases:

- **Health:** +20 points (10 hearts)
- **Armour:** +2 points (1 icon)
- **Attack damage:** +2 points (1 heart)
- **Flying speed:** +0.01
